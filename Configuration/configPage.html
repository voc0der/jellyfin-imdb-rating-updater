How It Works

This plugin downloads the official IMDb ratings flat file daily and updates the Community Rating field on all library items that have an IMDb ID. When enabled, season ratings are calculated as the average of eligible IMDb episode ratings in your library. No other metadata (titles, posters, cast, etc.) is modified.

The task runs on a daily schedule (default 3:00 AM) and can also be triggered manually from Dashboard → Scheduled Tasks.

Advanced Settings
When enabled, each season's Community Rating is set to the average of eligible IMDb episode ratings in your library. Episodes without an IMDb rating or below the minimum votes threshold are excluded. Only has effect when "Include TV Series" is turned on.
